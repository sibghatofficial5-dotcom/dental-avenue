Treatments, explained plainly

Every plan starts with a consultation, so you know what is needed, how long it takes and what it will cost before you decide.

  • Check-up and cleaning

    A thorough exam and polish to keep teeth and gums healthy, and to catch small problems early.

  • Teeth whitening

    Brighter teeth in a controlled, comfortable session that protects your enamel.

  • Smile design and veneers

    Shape, shade and proportion planned around your face, so the result looks like you.

  • Braces and aligners

    Straighter teeth with an option that fits your routine and your budget.

  • Fillings and root canal care

    Pain-managed treatment that saves your natural tooth wherever possible.

  • Implants and replacements

    Natural-looking solutions for missing teeth, planned with a clear timeline.
