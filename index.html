Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about visits, treatments, and appointments.

Is dental treatment at Dental Avenue painful? +

No. We specialize in pain-managed dentistry. Using modern topical gels and gentle local anaesthetic techniques, our procedures—from fillings to root canal treatments—are engineered to keep you relaxed and pain-free.

How do I book an appointment? +

You can book immediately through our WhatsApp number (0300 6119454) or submit the booking form below. We will confirm an exact time slot so you won't have to wait in a crowded waiting room.

How long does teeth whitening or smile design take? +

In-chair teeth whitening typically takes 45 to 60 minutes with immediate results. Custom smile design or veneers usually require 2 to 3 appointments to ensure perfect contouring, shading, and fitting.

Do you treat children and families? +

Yes, we are a full-scope family dental practice. We welcome patients of all ages and create a warm, non-intimidating environment for kids and adults alike.
